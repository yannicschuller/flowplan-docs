# Home page widgets

What each widget of the home page shows – and how **Focus & time** helps you work with focus and keep an eye on your hours.

How to add, move and set up widgets is described under [Customize your home page](/first-steps#customize-your-home-page). Every widget has a heading of its own in the gear and, where it fits, further settings.

| **Recently edited** | Pages edited lately – or, as **Recently viewed**, the ones you opened last. |
| **Favourites** | Starred pages and records. |
| **In your workspace** | The top-level pages of all spaces or one. |

| **Agenda** | The next days from calendar views and connected calendars. |

## Focus & time

The widget combines a focus timer in the style of the Pomodoro technique with your time tracking.

### Start a focus session

Without a session running, the widget shows buttons with minutes: your own length, 25 and 50 minutes (duplicates are left out). A click starts the session. You set the length in the gear under **Focus time in minutes** – 5 to 120, default 25.

While it runs, the widget shows **Focus** with the time left (minutes and seconds). Once it is over, a **Break** of 5 minutes starts by itself with its own countdown; after that the widget shows the start buttons again. **Stop** ends the focus time or the break at once.

At the end there is no sound and no notification in the browser – the widget just switches to the break. Start it in the app for iPhone or Android and the app shows the time left as a Live Activity or ongoing notification and follows the switch to the break ([Live Activities](/mobile-widgets#live-activities-and-ongoing-notifications)).

There is one focus session per person: start it on the home page and you also see it on your other devices and in the apps' widgets – and can stop it there.

### Time tracking and hours

- **Running timer**: when [time tracking](/projects-and-tickets#time-tracking) is running on a record, the widget shows its title and the time elapsed. A click on the title opens the record, **Stop time tracking** (the stop symbol) stops it.
- **Today · This week**: at the bottom are your hours tracked today and this week (Monday to today, in your time zone) – from all your time tracking, a running timer included. A session past midnight counts for both days.

The focus session itself tracks no time: only what runs through time tracking goes into the hours.
